Hello there!

I'm Tzeh Yuan, a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at Harvard University, supervised by Milind Tambe. I'm broadly interested in algorithmic game theory and sequential decision making.

Previously, I was research engineer at A*STAR Centre for Frontier AI Research (2024 - 2025), supervised by Basura Fernando. I completed my undergraduate degrees in Computer Science at the University of Oxford (2021 - 2024), where I worked with Edith Elkind and Elias Koutsoupias.
